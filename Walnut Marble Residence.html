WALNUT & MARBLE RESIDENCE

Rich walnut joinery forms the main character of this residence, balanced by expressive marble surfaces and soft, neutral furnishings. From the detailed kitchen and dining spaces to the quieter reading corner and living area, the interiors bring together classic detailing with a more contemporary sense of comfort. Carefully layered lighting, arched elements and herringbone flooring add depth throughout, giving the home a refined look without making it feel overly formal.
