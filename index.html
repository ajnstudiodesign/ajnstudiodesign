LITTLE HAVEN

Soft dusty-rose tones, playful details and plenty of natural light give this children's bedroom a cheerful yet considered character. The curved wardrobe fronts, fluted wall detailing and built-in study area add a sense of individuality, while the patterned rug, toys and checked textiles bring a more playful touch to the space. The neutral timber flooring and soft curtains keep the overall palette balanced, creating a room that feels comfortable, functional and made for everyday use.
